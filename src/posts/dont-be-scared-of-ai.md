Let’s break it down, no cap.

## First things first, what even is AI?

AI = Artificial Intelligence.

Basically, it’s like a super smart assistant that helps with tasks, writes code, debugs stuff, suggests solutions, and saves you HOURS.

But remember, it’s an assistant, not the CEO. You’re still the main brain.

## Should I use AI while learning to code?

If you’re just starting out — like still figuring out HTML from HTTP, don’t rely on AI too much. Here’s why:

- AI will give you the answer, but you won’t understand the “why” behind it
- If you keep copying-pasting code from day one, you’ll never build your problem-solving muscle
- You’ll miss out on real dev growth

Instead, use:

- **Docs** (yes, read the boring stuff)
- **YouTube** (free mentorship, kinda)
- **Stack Overflow** (classic dev trauma center)

Then once you’ve built 10–15 mini projects or got your hands dirty a bit, bring in AI to boost your speed + output.

## Okay, but how do YOU use AI as a developer?

Glad you asked. Here’s how I roll:

- **ChatGPT:** For brainstorming logic, fixing bugs, writing clean code
- **Cursor (with Claude):** My daily go-to for fast workflows
- **v0.dev:** For quickly building UI/landing pages
- **Grok & Perplexity:** For staying updated with new tech drops
- **GitHub Copilot (sometimes):** For auto-suggesting code in the flow

But again, I already knew how to code. I just use AI to level up my speed, optimize my code, and focus on the fun parts.

## So… will AI take dev jobs?

Short answer: No.

Long answer: It’ll take the jobs of devs who don’t adapt.

If you keep up with tech, learn how to use AI tools smartly, and build real skills, you’re unstoppable.

Companies don’t just want someone who can code. They want someone who’s fast, updated, and efficient.

> “It’s not AI vs Developers — it’s Developers + AI vs Time.”

- It’s not your enemy. It’s your cheat code if you know when & how to use it.
- Don’t depend on AI when you’re just starting out, build base skills first.

- Keep learning. Keep building. Stay updated.
- Be the dev who uses AI, not the dev who fears it.

So nah, AI ain’t taking your job bro, it’s just exposing the folks who stopped learning.

Use it right and you’re a weapon.
